I build a lot and I remember almost none of it.

Not the code — the code is in git. I mean the thinking. Why I picked one thing
over another, what I was scared of the week before it worked, the specific dumb
mistake that cost me a month. That part evaporates. A year from now I will have
shipped a dozen things and be unable to tell you what any of them taught me.

So: this. One entry whenever I actually learn something, sent to whoever wants
it. Not a content strategy. A ledger.

## The rules I am giving myself

1. **Write it the week it happens.** A lesson written six months late is a
   lesson invented six months late.
2. **Specifics or nothing.** No "work hard and stay consistent." What broke,
   what I did, what it cost.
3. **Publish before it is good.** Being cringe is never cringe.

## The long game

Every entry is a plain markdown file sitting in the repo for this site. That is
on purpose. In a year there will be fifty of them, and fifty essays with a
through-line is not a blog anymore — it is a first draft.

That is the actual plan. The book is the point. This is just me refusing to lose
the notes on the way there.

If you want them as I write them, the box is at the top of this page.
